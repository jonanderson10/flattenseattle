Cost weights

The cost of a street, in equivalent metres, is length + α×climb + β×steep-distance penalties + γ×extreme-grade penalty. Move the sliders to see the route respond.

Using custom weights rather than a preset.

The warped city

Redraw San Francisco so that distance on the page is climbing cost instead of geography. Places separated by a ridge move apart; places joined by a flat corridor pull together.

Flatness weight λ
0 is plain walking distance; 1 is the balanced objective; higher values make hills loom larger. Keep it modest to avoid absurd detours.
Morph
Street vertices, neighborhood outlines, corridors and your route are all carried by the same deformation, so the route you see is the route you would walk.
